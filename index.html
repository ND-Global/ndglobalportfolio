06 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's the typical project timeline?

Most single-brand websites take 3–5 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope. E‑commerce and larger multi-page systems usually run 6–8 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline before any work begins.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes — I work with clients across India and internationally. All collaboration happens over calls, email and shared docs, so location isn't a constraint.

What's included after the site goes live?

Every project includes a short post-launch support window to fix any issues. Ongoing maintenance, content updates and new features can continue on a simple monthly or per-request basis.

What platforms and tech do you build with?

Mainly Next.js, React and Tailwind CSS for custom builds, and Shopify for e‑commerce stores that need a managed backend. The stack is chosen based on what the project actually needs — not a one-size-fits-all template.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on scope — a brand website is priced differently from an e‑commerce store or a booking system. Share a few details about your project and I'll send a clear, itemised quote.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. I regularly rebuild dated or underperforming sites — keeping the content and SEO value you've already earned while modernising the design and speed.
